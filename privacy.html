Shipping & returns Privacy Terms

This page explains what we collect about you, why we collect it, and who sees it.

Who we are

LATITO is a small, Australian-owned skincare brand on the Gold Coast, Queensland. In this policy, “we” and “us” means LATITO.

We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988.

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, email, phone number, delivery address and what you bought. You pay on Shopify’s secure checkout. We never see your full card number.
  • When you join the Founding 100: your email address.
  • When you email us: your email address and what you tell us.
  • When you visit the site: your bag is saved in your own browser, so it’s still there when you come back. When our ad tools are switched on, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok use cookies and pixels. They record things like the pages you view, what you add to your bag, your device, your browser and your IP address.

We don’t ask for health details. If you tell us about a skin reaction, we only use it to help you and sort out your refund.

Why we collect it

  • To make, pack and ship your order, and send you tracking.
  • To answer your emails and handle refunds, including the 30-night promise.
  • To send you emails, but only if you signed up.
  • To see which ads work, once our ad tools are on.
  • To keep the tax and business records the law asks for.

Who we share it with

We only share what each one needs to do its job.

  • Shopify runs our shop and checkout, and takes your payment.
  • Our fulfilment partner makes and ships your order. They get your name, delivery address and phone number so your parcel can reach you.
  • Delivery companies carry your parcel. They may text or email you about it.
  • Klaviyo sends our emails, if you signed up.
  • Meta and TikTok measure our ads, when our ad tools are switched on.

We don’t sell your personal information. We share it with others only if the law says we must.

Information sent overseas

Our products ship from our manufacturing partner overseas. So your name, address and phone number go to that partner in its country.

Shopify, Klaviyo, Meta and TikTok may also store or use your information outside Australia, such as in the USA and Canada.

Cookies and pixels

A cookie is a small file your browser keeps. A pixel is a small piece of code. It tells an ad company what happened on a page.

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The shop still works, but your bag may not be saved.
  • You can change the ads you see in your Facebook, Instagram and TikTok settings.

Emails from us

We only send marketing emails if you sign up. Every email has an unsubscribe link. One click and you’re off the list. Or email us and we’ll take you off.

We still send emails about your order, like your receipt and tracking.

Keeping it safe

We use well-known services with strong security. Only people who need your details can see them.

We keep order records for as long as tax law needs, usually five years. Then we delete them.

See or fix your details

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you. You can ask us to fix it if it’s wrong. You can also ask us to delete it, unless the law says we must keep it, like tax records.

Just email us. It’s free. We’ll reply within 30 days.

Questions or complaints

Email us at latito.nm@gmail.com. A real person reads it. We’ll reply within 30 days.

Not happy with our answer? You can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC). Visit oaic.gov.au or call 1300 363 992.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the last change.
