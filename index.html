Before you buy

Loved the hype. Hated the peel?

Most overnight masks don’t fail. Nobody shows you how to use them. Here’s what goes wrong, and how to get it right.

  1. “It flaked off in tiny crumbs.”

    The layer was too thin. Brush on one thick, even coat. Think icing, not primer. The brush makes it easy.

  2. “Half of it ended up on my pillow.”

    It needs 15–20 minutes to dry. Put it on, then scroll your phone. Go to bed once it’s fully dry.

  3. “It pulled my baby hairs.”

    Keep it off your hairline, brows and under your nose. Peel slowly, from the jaw up. Rinse off what’s left.

  4. “The cream felt too heavy.”

    Mix your own ratio. Oily skin? Two parts gel, one part capsules. Dry skin? Flip it. A pea-sized bit does your whole face.

  5. “I broke out.”

    New products need an intro. Patch test behind your ear first. Then use it every second night for a week.
