Where your order ships from, how long it takes, and how our 30-night promise works.

Shipping

  • Orders leave within 1–2 business days. They arrive in 7–12 business days, with tracking.
  • Free tracked shipping over $45. Otherwise $7.95.
  • Every parcel is tracked. We email your tracking as soon as it ships.
  • LATITO is Australian-owned. Our products are made by a partner overseas. Your parcel ships from our manufacturing partner, straight to your door.
  • Business days are Monday to Friday. Public holidays don’t count.
  • We ship to addresses in Australia.

These times are our best guide. Busy times or customs can slow a parcel down. If yours hasn’t arrived by the end of the window, email us your order number. We’ll chase it for you.

Please check your address at checkout. Spot a mistake? Email us straight away. Once your parcel ships, we may not be able to change it.

Our 30-night promise

Use it for up to 30 nights. Not happy with how your skin looks? Email us within 45 days of delivery. Send a photo of the product, even if it’s open or empty. We’ll refund what you paid. You don’t need to send it back.

To claim, email latito.nm@gmail.com with your order number and the photo.

Your rights under Australian Consumer Law

Our 30-night promise is on top of your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. It doesn’t replace them or take anything away.

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

Damaged, faulty or wrong item

Sorry about that. Email us at latito.nm@gmail.com as soon as you can. Send your order number and a photo of the item. We’ll send a new one or refund you.

Refunds

Refunds go back to your original payment method, like your card. Once we approve a refund, your bank may take a few business days to show it.
